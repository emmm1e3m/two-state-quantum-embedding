Population-order reversal at fixed actual disturbance $\eps=P(0|1)$, shown as $\Delta/\eps$ with $\Delta=\eps-a$. The horizontal coordinate is $L_\eps=\log(1/\eps)$; the upper axis gives $\eps$. Solid curves with circles and squares show the tilted pure-stationary-state construction, Eqs.~\eqref{eq:familyparams}--\eqref{eq:familyjump}, and the diagonal-stationary-state construction, Eqs.~\eqref{eq:diag-jump}--\eqref{eq:diag-xstar}, respectively. Dashed curves show the leading laws $4/(eL_\eps)$ and $64/(e^2L_\eps^2)$. Both axes are logarithmic. The solid curves are attainable lower bounds for the corresponding optimal gains; markers distinguish the constructions.
